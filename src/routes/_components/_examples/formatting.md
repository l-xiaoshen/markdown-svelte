# Text formatting

Use **bold**, _italic_, ~~strikethrough~~, `inline code`, and [links](https://svelte.dev) in a paragraph.

## Blockquotes

> A quoted paragraph.
>
> A second paragraph in the same blockquote.

::: tip
Blank lines separate paragraphs.
:::

## Additional syntax

- Highlight: ==highlighted text==.
- Subscript: H~2~O. Superscript: x^2^.
- Footnote reference.[^detail]

---

Math is displayed as source, inline $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$ or in a block:

$$
E = mc^2
$$

[^detail]: Footnotes are collected at the end of the document.
